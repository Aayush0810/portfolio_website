The Profile

Connecting long-term capital directly with high-quality projects.

Dushyant Khanna is Founder and Managing Director of Panache Capital, a Melbourne-headquartered investment bank specialising in large-scale structured financing for real estate, infrastructure and energy assets.

Across more than two decades in investment banking, structured finance and strategic investment, he has built a practice centred on originating and executing large-ticket cross-border transactions — with Panache Capital having arranged more than US$10 billion in financing.

He maintains principal-level relationships with sovereign wealth funds, family offices and institutional lenders in more than fifteen countries, connecting capital with the projects that shape economies.
